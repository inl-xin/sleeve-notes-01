GREEN IN THE EVENING

Three frogs, one pond, and a rhythm section that swings like reeds in a night wind. A bullfrog walks the bass line, a tree frog comps bright chords like crickets after dark, and the drummer brushes lily pads soft as rain — every chorus ends with the whole pond singing along. Recorded live at the water's edge: no overdubs, no flies on the wall, just the trio at dusk.
